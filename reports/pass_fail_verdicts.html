Read this carefully before repeating either number as "confirmed." The +2.61% figure is real and honestly computed, but its 95% CI is [−5.34%, +10.55%] — it does not clear this project's own gate rule, and should be described as promising, execution-focused evidence from a small (696-pick), single-week sample, not a proven edge. The +1.32% figure is the one result in this entire audit that genuinely clears n≥500 AND CI-lower-bound > 0 on its own — CI [0.19%, 2.45%] — but it weakened when a 4th real season (2023) was added (was +1.93%, CI [0.54%, 3.33%] on 3 seasons), and a "consistent upward trend across seasons" argument used earlier to support it did not survive 2023 being added (2023 alone is flat, and the tighter −249..−150 sub-band actually reverses negative in 2023). Still the most defensible "real positive ROI" claim in this audit — just a more marginal one than first reported. Full derivation: MILESTONE_1_GATE_REPORT.md, Addendums 10, 12, 14.

Why production underperforms even the flat baseline

The +2.61% figure above comes from this investigation (Addendum 12): holding 696 identical real picks fixed (same player, game, line, outcome) and swapping only the recorded price.

78% of matched picks got a measurably worse price than what the market actually offered at the time — a ~19-point ROI swing from price alone, big enough to explain the entire live production shortfall on its own. No bookmaker field is currently logged, so the exact source can't be traced yet; that's the single highest-leverage fix identified in this whole audit.
