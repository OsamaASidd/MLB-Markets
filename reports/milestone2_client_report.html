What this audit ruled out

The 7 fails above are not a lack of effort — they're the result of testing every reasonable angle and reporting honestly when nothing held up. That discipline is what makes the 4 passes trustworthy.

New client data

Pulled splits, bullpen state, park geometry, and pitch-mix data not previously available, and re-tested every failing market with it.

Model diversity

Classification, Poisson/regression on the actual outcome count, and cross-market composite features — not just one algorithm searched harder.

Honest holdouts

Every promising near-miss was re-tested on data the model had never touched before being counted. Several evaporated under this test — reported as fails, not hidden.

Bias hypothesis testing

Tested directly for favorite-longshot market mispricing on H2H/Totals. Not present in this data — ruled out explicitly, not assumed away.

Bet-sizing analysis

Kelly-criterion staking on the confirmed passes shows real edges are thin enough that full Kelly is dangerous — fractional staking is the practical recommendation.

One fixed rule

The same gate rule, the same single pre-specified betting cut, applied to every market — no threshold shopping after the fact, anywhere in this audit.
