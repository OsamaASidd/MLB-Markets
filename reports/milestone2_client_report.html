What this audit ruled out

The 7 fails above are not a lack of effort — they're the result of testing every reasonable angle and reporting honestly when nothing held up. That discipline is what makes the 4 passes trustworthy.

New client data

Pulled splits, bullpen state, park geometry, pitch-mix, and live odds-snapshot history not previously available, and re-tested every failing market with it.

Model diversity

Classification, Poisson/regression on the actual outcome count, cross-market composite features, and a market-offset ridge-logistic GLM matching the client's own live production architecture (Fernando GLM) — four distinct model families per market, not one algorithm searched harder.

Honest holdouts

Every promising near-miss was re-tested on data the model had never touched before being counted. Several evaporated under this test — reported as fails, not hidden.

Real bugs found and fixed

Two join fan-out bugs (inflating a previously-published FAIL by ~27%) and a lookahead-leakage bug (a season-long final stat used to "predict" mid-season games) were found and corrected across four market pipelines. Verdicts didn't change; the underlying numbers now do — and are more trustworthy for it.

Bet-sizing analysis

Kelly-criterion staking on the confirmed passes shows real edges are thin enough that full Kelly is dangerous — fractional staking is the practical recommendation.

One fixed rule, transparently revised

The same gate rule, the same single pre-specified betting cut, applied to every market — no threshold shopping after the fact. The one rule change made (adding MONITORING) was proposed with its exact threshold disclosed in advance and approved by the client before any result was reclassified.
